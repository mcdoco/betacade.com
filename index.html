How it works

1Set up your game page

A title, a description, a cover image and a web build, with a Play now button and a history of every update. Do it yourself in the studio, or have your agent do it: it goes from sign-up to a playable, branded page in under a minute.

2Share one link

Players open it in the browser, on a phone or a laptop. No install, no account.

3See what your players did

Live players, progress from their saves, errors with what led up to them, their notes, and a replay of each session you can scrub through.

{% include mock-replay.html %}

4Quash bugs

Feedback, errors and bugs land on one board, each with the moment it happened. Turn a note into a bug and move it along as you fix it.

{% include mock-board.html %}

5Find the fun

Reply to players by their nickname, tell them when it's fixed, and hear what they make of the new build. You never see their email; they never see yours.

For players

Playing is free, in the browser, with no account.

Your saves follow you.

Sign in with your email and your saves come with you to any device. Pick up where you left off, switch between saves, or go back to an earlier one.

Anonymous to studios.

Studios see your progress under a made-up name, like Amber Fox, never your name or email. You choose whether a game sends anything at all, and you can turn it off any time.

Your data, your call.

See what a studio sees about you, and delete your play data for any game whenever you like.
