How it works

1Set up your game page

A title, a description, a cover image and a web build. Players get a play page that opens with a welcome card, and if you like, an About page with screenshots that search engines can find. Do it yourself in the studio, or have your agent do it.

Put this game on Betacade. Start at https://api.betacade.com/llms.txt MAKE NO MISTAKES.

2Share one link

Players open it in the browser, on a phone or a laptop. No install, no account.

{% include mock-devices.html %}

3See what your players did

Live players, progress from their saves, errors with what led up to them, their notes, and a replay of each session you can scrub through.

{% include mock-replay.html %}

4Quash bugs

Feedback lands on one board beside your issues, each with the moment it happened. Turn a note into an issue and move it along, from Issues to In progress to Ready to test, then Fixed or Won't fix. Players who reported it are told which build fixed it.

{% include mock-board.html %}

5Find the fun

Reply to players by their nickname, tell them when it's fixed, and hear what they make of the new build. You never see their email; they never see yours.

Meet your players where they are

Run your playtests and the community around your game together, without moving anyone off Discord.

Sign in with Discord.

Players and studios can sign in with Discord, or link it to the account they already have.

Your Discord, on your game.

Add your server's invite and players see "Join the Pond Studio Discord" on the welcome and in the pause menu. Give the Discord its own tracked link to see how many players came from it.

Notes from Discord.

If your game's channel is on the Betacade Discord, right-click a message there, pick Apps, then Send to Betacade, and it lands in your game's inbox. Roles there show who plays (Playtester) and who makes games (Studio).

Join the Betacade Discord

Work as a team

Invite people by email. Viewers are free on any plan; developers need Pro.

Viewers and developers.

Viewers see dashboards, playtests, replays and feedback. Developers also deploy, change settings and reply to testers. Up to 3 people on Free, 10 on Pro.

Who did what.

The dashboard says who shipped each build, and a tester's thread who wrote each reply. Players never see who's in the studio, only its name.

Private builds.

Give a build to one group of people while everyone else keeps playing the public one. Replays and Rewind work on it too. On Pro.

Show it off

For when your game is ready for a crowd.

Leaderboards and achievements.

A leaderboard of the scores your game sends, with names only for players who opt in. Achievements are rules over the saves that Betacade checks, so players can't award themselves.

Live pages.

Put a page on a big screen at an event: a QR code to play, a running scoreboard, and replays of the best runs.

Jams.

Make a jam page and share its code. Games join, players play and vote for their top 3, and the results show when voting closes.

Built for your agent

Claude Code, Cursor or Codex can do the whole loop.

One prompt.

Give your agent the prompt above. It signs in once with a code you approve, uploads the build and hands back the link. Files that haven't changed aren't uploaded again.

One guide.

llms.txt covers deploying, setting up the page, reading who played and what they said, and filing bugs. Every answer from the API tells the agent what to do next.

A versioned API.

Every route is versioned and in the OpenAPI spec. The changelog marks anything that breaks and says how to migrate.

For players

Playing is free, in the browser, with no account.

Your saves follow you.

Sign in with your email or Discord and your saves come with you to any device. Pick up where you left off, switch between saves, or go back to an earlier one.

Anonymous to studios.

Studios see your progress under a made-up name, like Amber Fox, never your name or email. You choose whether a game sends anything at all, and you can turn it off any time.

Your data, your call.

See what a studio sees about you, and delete your play data for any game whenever you like.
